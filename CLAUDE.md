## Visual layer (added Nov 2026)
- `world/industrial_models.gd`: detailed batch ovens and chocolate enrobers (Oven/Oven02, Coater/Coater02). Built from primitive meshes; PBR textures (brushed steel, paint, ribbed cladding, wire belt, vents, hazard stripes) are generated in code at startup, because downloads are not available and to keep the project asset-free. Static parts are merged per material via `Primitives.batch_static`; fine details skip shadows and stop drawing beyond `DETAIL_RANGE` (170 m, the whole hall; it was 48 m and details popped in only when close), plate text beyond 48 m.
- Enrober motion (belt texture scroll, sprocket, coated pieces, chocolate curtain) is driven by the coater's `total_output`, not by real time, so it follows the simulation. The curtain takes the order's `coating_color`.
- `world/hall_models.gd`: hall lighting and exponential haze, 9 m clad walls, open truss roof with high-bay lamps, additive floor light pools (no real lights), concrete floor material. Sims-style cutaway: walls facing the camera switch to shadows-only, and roof bays between the camera and its focus point are hidden. Driven by `Floor.update_view(camera)` from `scenes/factory.gd` `_process`; it no-ops when the camera hasn't moved.
- Performance rule from the owner: performance comes first, and the machine must not run hot. Prefer emissive materials and merged meshes over real lights, transparency and per-frame updates.
- `world/industrial_models.gd` is also the shared model kit: public helpers (`box`, `cylinder`, `pipe`, `leg`, `handrail`, `caster`, `drive_motor`, `stack_light`, `finish`, …) and materials (`stainless`, `polished`, `grating`, `rubber`, `paint(color)`, …). Build new equipment with it and call `finish(root)` so static parts merge per material.
- `world/line_models.gd`: extruder, ingredient hopper, cream mixer, roller conveyors, palletizing table, wire and long-span shelving. `world/processing_models.gd`: spreader, cutter, cooling tunnel and flow wrappers on a shared stainless conveyor with a scrolling belt. Their pivot names (`MeteringRoller`, `CuttingHead`, `CoolingFan`, `SealingJaw`, `FeedReel`) are checked by `tests/product_standards_test.gd`.
- Workers, carts and the forklift (`world/logistics_view.gd`), pallets/cartons/sacks/wafers (`world/stock_models.gd`, generated wood, cardboard, woven and waffle textures) and the warehouse racking (`world/warehouse_view.gd`) were rebuilt in Nov 2026. Workers are no longer built from primitives: they are skinned GLB models (`assets/characters/worker_*.glb`, four variants) made in Blender from free CC0 Quaternius packs. See `CREDITS.md` for sources and licences, and `tools/blender/` for the rebuild scripts (Blender 5.3 is at `/Applications/Blender.app`). `logistics_view._pose_worker` picks a clip from the operator state (Idle, Walk, Carry, Carry_Idle, Cart_Push, PickUp_Table), scales its speed to ground speed, and pauses the AnimationPlayer for idle workers and while the simulation is paused. This matters: with 65 bones, five meshes and always-on players the crew cost about 17 ms of render time per frame; the 23-bone single-mesh models with idle pausing cost about 0.7 ms. Keep the skeleton small and never leave idle players active. The carried sack rides on the `spine_03` bone. Vest and cap materials are tinted per role at runtime. Clips ending `_Loop` lose that suffix on import.
- FG cartons are printed per product: `assets/cartons/<code>.png` atlases (brand front, flavour end, taped top) from `tools/cartons/build_cartons.py`, VRAM-compressed. `Stock.pallet` draws a pallet's cartons as one MultiMesh (`visible_instance_count` = boxes shown) with `printed_board(sku, tint)`; products without art fall back to `generic.png` tinted by package colour. A new FG product needs a design in the script. `Stock.carton()` stays plain kraft for props and packaging supplies.
- `world/gum_models.gd`: Z-blade gum mixer, rotating coating pans (open polished drum, charge tinted to the order) and IBC syrup totes. Sheeter, fruit former, wallet packer, bottle filler and gum conditioning are modes of `world/processing_models.gd`.
- `world/site_models.gd`: continuous walkways (cut only by machine clearance), machine safety zones, department signs, QC lab, office and workshop as one wall-sharing block on the rear wall, changing room and canteen sharing a wall against the finished goods warehouse's west wall (`_staff_rooms`; the pallet store, kitting and marshalling no longer fit the 120×75 plan and are skipped), kitting, marshalling, safety points and seeded loose cartons. Wall service pipes are in `world/hall_models.gd` (`_pipe_runs`) so they share the wall cutaway. Cart dock squares and the cart-aisle paint were removed at the owner's request. Built every `Floor.configure`; each item is checked against equipment, docks and the live cart/worker/forklift/shuttle routes and skipped where it would sit on one. All parts merge into one node.
- Downloaded props (`assets/props/*.glb`: extinguisher, water cooler, exit sign, call point, first-aid kit, wet-floor sign, cone; Poly Pizza, credited in `CREDITS.md`) are normalised by `tools/blender/build_safety_props.py` (front +Z, base or back-face origin, real size). `site_models._model()` splits each into single-material meshes marked mergeable so they batch with the rest of the site. Grid y runs along world -Z, so a prop against the back wall (max y) takes yaw 0.
- `world/grounds_models.gd` (Nov 2026): the grounds outside the hall, all relative to the grid so any plan size works. Public road and main gate (front-right) with a security gatehouse on a kerbed island, a canopy, boom barriers and an open sliding gate; ring road; staff car park in front (about 50 cars, seeded); staff garden with a pond and fountain; truck docks with articulated lorries on the outside of the hall wall wherever a warehouse touches it; trailer park on the east side; perimeter mesh fence and tree belts. Vehicles and plants are `assets/site/*.glb` (Poly Pizza, see `CREDITS.md`; built by `tools/blender/build_site_props.py`, which also thins the trees). Car paint is recoloured through the `<name>_paint` material. Parts merge per material in eight zones (`GroundsBase`, `GroundsFront`, …). Dock doors and shelters are added to the hall wall node (`Hall.wall_structure`) so they follow the cutaway; since that wall is cut away whenever the camera could see its outside, they mostly show as shadow. Bumpers and the hazard dock edge sit in the zone and stay visible. The sky colour now matches the fog (`#a9b4ba`), and `OrbitCamera.SITE_MARGIN` lets the camera pan over the grounds. Measured: about 460k triangles, home view uncapped about 145 FPS on the M4 Pro.
- Everything in `world/` is visual only and must never write simulation state.

## Agent interface (added Nov 2026)
- `agent/` exposes the ERP and simulation to AI planning agents: `agent_api.gd` (JSON commands), `work_center_monitor.gd` (read-only state-time buckets), `server.gd` (TCP, newline JSON) and `mcp_bridge.py` (stdlib-only MCP stdio bridge, registered in `.mcp.json`). Scenarios live in `scenarios/`.
- Live mode: `godot --path . -- --agent-port 8765` skips the opening page and starts `agent/agent_link.gd` inside `scenes/factory.gd`. The API then attaches to the game's line (`attach`), time commands reply from `live_step` after the game has played them, and `on_line_replaced` hands scenario loads and restores back to the scene. Autosave on close is off while the link is open. The link refuses `shutdown` in the game.
- Agent actions must go through the same ERP functions as the UI. Never give the agent a shortcut that bypasses release checks, reservations or the ledger.
- Command argument specs follow `"[optional ]<string|number|int|bool|array>[: note]"`. The bridge builds MCP schemas from them.
- Run `tests/agent_api_test.gd` after changing anything in `agent/`, ERP command signatures, MRP output, or order/demand fields.

## MRP (phase 1, Nov 2026)
- `erp/stage_mrp.gd` holds explicit-stage MRP: lot sizing (EX/FX/PB, min/max/rounding), safety time, exception messages and the coverage summary. Planned orders persist in `book.planned_orders` (`PLO-…`, `planned_sequence`); unfirmed ones are regenerated each run and keep their number by planning key, firmed ones are fixed supply. `erp/mrp.gd` adds `find`, `convert_all`, `firm` and `change`. The legacy combined-route path in `mrp.gd` is unchanged.
- Material settings: optional `planning` object in `materials.json`, read through `catalog.planning(code)`, validated by `catalog.set_planning`. Finished goods must stay lot-for-lot (demand linking assumes one order per demand).
- Run `tests/mrp_planning_test.gd` after touching MRP, planned orders or material planning settings.
- Known gap (phase 2): MRP plans with unlimited capacity while conversion schedules finitely, so converted orders can land later than MRP assumed. Exceptions report it; nothing levels it yet.

## Raw-material warehouse capacity (Nov 2026)
- RM-WH holds 90 pallet positions (`master_data/storage_capacity.json`), sized to about one full-rate 24 h day (about 82 positions). Purchased materials carry `storage` (`rack`/`tank`/`pallet_store`/`shelf`) and racked ones `pallet_qty`. Helpers: `catalog.positions`, `catalog.pallet_qty`, `catalog.raw_capacity`, `operations.raw_room`, `operations.raw_warehouse`.
- Receipts put away only what fits; the rest waits as `po.at_dock`. MRP raises `storage_full` and `at_dock`. The rack view (`factory_floor._refresh_raw_slots`) draws one instanced pallet per occupied position.
- Opening stock must fit (catalog validation). Run `tests/raw_warehouse_test.gd` after touching storage, receipts or pallet sizes.

## Simulation instances and wording (Nov 2026)
- The project has no work shifts. A run is a **simulation instance**: never call it a "shift" in UI or docs. ("Three shifts" in the warehouse sizing means real work shifts.)
- `scenes/start_menu.tscn` (`ui/start_menu.gd`) is the main scene: create, continue and rename instances. `erp/instances.gd` keeps one save per instance in `user://instances/` plus `index.json`, imports the old single save (`SaveGame.PATH`) once as "My factory" without deleting it, and hands the chosen instance to `scenes/factory.gd` through `Instances.pending`.
- The factory scene saves only when it runs an instance (`_instance_id`). Opened directly (tests, `--new-instance`, `--agent-port`), it is an unsaved run. ERP has Save; the factory bottom bar has Main menu (with a confirmation); the single-slot Load button is gone. The opening page can rename and delete (with confirmation) instances. The agent saves with `save_instance`.
- The Reset button was removed. Crew/cart counts apply through `Line.sync_fleet()` once no order runs. `line.reset()` remains for the sandbox mode and tests.
- Tests must set `Instances.directory` to their own folder (see `tests/instances_test.gd`) so they never touch the player's instances. Run that suite after touching instances, the opening page or save/close handling.

## Floor plan (Nov 2026)
- The default plan (`master_data/factory_layout.json`, 120 × 75 m, plan `aisle-v1`) is a double-loaded production aisle: north row turns 0, south row turns 2 with input/output dock x offsets swapped. Lanes are layout data (`layout.cart_lane`, `walk_lane`, `supply_lane`, `lift_lane`); never use the `Layout.*_LANE` constants outside legacy defaults.
- Saves without `lanes` are the original plan: `Layout.new(size, "", snapshot)` flags them `legacy` and reads `factory_layout_legacy.json`. `Layout.new(Vector2i.ZERO, "", {}, true)` builds the original plan. Fixtures cut from old saves must use it. Layout files carry `plan` and are refused across plans.
- Tune the plan with the validator: `Layout.validate()` names the first failing dock, route or belt. Port offsets (supply stops, dock x swaps for turned machines) are data in the JSON.
- Run `tests/floor_plan_test.gd` and `tests/layout_editor_test.gd` after touching layouts or lanes.

## FG warehouse racks (Nov 2026)
- The FG warehouse holds 96 pallets: 24 ground positions × 4 levels (floor, then beams at 1.95, 3.90, 5.82 m; `WarehouseRoom.FG_LEVEL_HEIGHTS`). Slot index = `level * 24 + position`, so old 24-slot saves pad to 96 and keep their positions; slots fill floor-first (`warehouse.find(null)`).
- Layout: `warehouse_slot_count()` is the capacity, `warehouse_position_count()` the ground positions routes use, `warehouse_level/height(index)`. Forklift routes and approaches are per position and shared by all levels.
- The forklift's drop-off takes `Line.storage_handling_seconds(slot)` = 2 s + 1.5 s per level (`LIFT_LEVEL_SECONDS`); `logistics_view` raises the forks and an inner mast section to the level. Stored pallets and their pick boxes sit at the level height.
- Dispatch is still instant (no retrieval trip), so only storing is timed.

## Stage overlap (Nov 2026)
- Stages run together: a stage with `start_kg` (scheduling.json) releases on a first lot of its manufactured input plus released upstream cover. `ERP.shortages`, `start_quantity`, `pending_output`, `outlook`; `Scheduling.overlaps/first_output/supply_late`. Reservations can exceed stock until upstream delivers. Extrusion and purchased inputs keep the full-quantity rule. Old saves have no `overlap` data and keep the old rule. Turn it off with `overlap.enabled`, `stage_overlap` in scenarios, or `set_stage_overlap`.
- Run `tests/stage_overlap_test.gd` after touching release checks, reservations, planned intervals or MRP netting.

## Crew pace (Nov 2026)
- `floor_speed` and `platform_speed` (1.4 m/s) and the handler's `MATERIAL_HANDLER_SPEED` are unladen walking. `Operator.carry_pace/cart_pace/jack_pace` scale them by the load (production_line `_advance_platform/_advance_floor`, `ERP.supply_tick`). Old saves with the untouched 2.4/1.8 defaults are migrated once (`save_game._migrate_walking_pace`, marker `pace`). Run `tests/worker_pace_test.gd` after touching crew movement.

## Material handler desk (Nov 2026)
- In plans with a high-bay raw warehouse the handler waits at a computer desk against the warehouse's west wall (`Layout.has_handler_desk/handler_desk/handler_jack_spot`), not at the dock. He walks to the parked pallet jack, works, parks it again and walks back (`ERP._handler_home`); `supply_route` joins the desk pocket to the dock. The jack is a top-level node in `logistics_view` that follows him only while he works with it. Tests that expect an immediate pickup must give him time to walk from the desk.

## Things to know
- `simulation/production_line.gd` (~1,800 lines) mixes three execution modes (legacy sandbox, combined routes, explicit stages) kept for save compatibility and regressions. Change it carefully.
- The simulation is fully deterministic: no breakdowns, random cycle times or supplier variability yet. The owner plans to use it for experiments, so any randomness should be seeded and optional.
- Demo timings are much faster than real life (short bakes, 60–90 s supplier lead times).
- About 22 existing GDScript warnings (variable shadowing, unused parameters) come from ERP/UI scripts and predate the visual work.
- Commit a checkpoint before large changes.
